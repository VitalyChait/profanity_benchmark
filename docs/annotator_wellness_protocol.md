# Annotator Wellness Protocol

## Before starting

- Read annotation guidelines and threat model
- Complete wellness briefing with annotation lead
- Acknowledge right to opt out without penalty

## During work

- **Exposure limits:** max 2 hours continuous harmful content per session
- **Paid breaks:** 15 min per hour mandatory
- **Difficulty tiers:** annotators may request reassignment from severity-3 queue
- **No model agreement pressure:** labels reflect guidelines, not model outputs

## Support resources

- Designated wellness contact (annotation lead)
- Institution counseling referral list
- Pause workflow: flag conversation for lead review without completing

## Prohibited

- Sharing conversation text outside secure annotation platform
- Discussing specific examples on social media
- Youth advisors annotating harm labels or severe sexual content

## Incident response

Report distressing content (CSAM, emergencies, doxxing) to lead immediately.
Stop work on flagged item; incident logged per IRB protocol.
