# Youth Advisory Protocol Draft (13–17)

**Requires IRB approval with guardian consent + youth assent.**

## Scope

Youth advisors review **sanitized** platform scenarios and contemporary language interpretations.
They do **not**:

- Produce explicit abuse
- Inspect severe sexual content
- Assign moderation labels
- Review unredacted harmful examples

## Activities

1. Scenario naturalness review (synthetic/staged cards without graphic content)
2. Algospeak / slang interpretation workshops
3. Sanitized submissions of emerging coded terms (no targeting individuals)
4. Feedback on annotation guideline clarity for youth communication

## Safeguards

- Guardian consent + youth assent documented
- Sessions facilitated by youth-safety-trained staff
- Materials pre-screened by adult team
- Compensation at institutional youth-advisory rates
- No publication claim that advisors authored benchmark harm examples

## Fallback

If IRB unavailable: replace with 18–24 advisors and narrow claims to
"youth-oriented communication settings."
